Our Process

From discovery to delivery — clearly defined.

A five-step workflow that keeps projects organized, communicative and accountable. Your role at each step is stated clearly.

Workflow

Five steps, one clear path.

01

Discovery

We start by understanding your business goals, target audience, requirements, constraints and project scope. Nothing is designed until this is clear.

Your Role

Provide required information, goals and references.

Output

Agreed project brief.

02

Strategy

We define direction, priorities, deliverables, timeline and execution approach. Scope is confirmed before creative work begins.

Your Role

Approve direction and scope.

Output

Agreed strategy/plan.

03

Creative Development

Concepts, design directions, content assets and campaign materials are produced. Revisions are handled within the agreed scope.

Your Role

Provide consolidated feedback and approvals.

Output

Drafts and agreed revisions.

04

Execution

Approved materials are implemented — campaign setup, content preparation, or publishing coordination. Access and approvals are managed upfront.

Your Role

Provide required access, approvals and assets.

Output

Approved execution-ready deliverables.

05

Delivery

Final files, handover, agreed documentation and — where applicable — reporting. Project closure is confirmed by both sides.

Your Role

Final review and handover confirmation.

Output

Final deliverables per agreed scope.

Important: Timelines and revision policies vary by project. We do not promise specific delivery times or unlimited revisions unless those terms are defined in a written agreement.
