Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Overview

This website is operated by ARZESH. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how it is used. It is a static website — there is no user account system, no contact form and no backend server.

2. Information You Share Directly

If you contact us via email or Facebook, you voluntarily provide whatever information you choose to include — such as your name, business name, project details and any attachments. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and to discuss potential collaboration.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Information Collected Automatically

This website does not currently use analytics tools, tracking pixels, advertising trackers or cookie-consent systems. No cookies are set by ARZESH for tracking purposes.

Like most websites, hosting providers may automatically log basic technical information (such as browser type, IP address and access time) for security and operational purposes. This logging is outside ARZESH's direct control and depends on the hosting provider's policies.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

This website uses or links to third-party services, including:

  • Google Fonts — for typography. Google Fonts may log basic request information according to Google's privacy policy.
  • Unsplash / Pexels — for photography images. Their servers may log image requests.
  • GitHub Pages — as the hosting provider.
  • Facebook — as a contact channel.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. ARZESH is not responsible for the privacy practices of external services.

5. How Information Is Used

Information you share directly is used only for:

  • Responding to your inquiry
  • Discussing project scope, timelines and pricing
  • Delivering agreed services if a project proceeds

6. Data Retention

Email and message exchanges are kept for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or longer if a business relationship continues. You may request deletion of past communication at any time (see Section 8).

7. Security

Reasonable care is taken to handle communication securely. However, no email or messaging system is completely secure. Please avoid sending highly sensitive information (such as passwords, financial account details or identity documents) unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information

Contact: arzesh.agency@gmail.com

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in the future, for example if tracking tools or forms are added to the website. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email arzesh.agency@gmail.com .
